ՈԼՈՐ is a 5×5 word-path puzzle in Armenian. Four words are hidden on the board —
one of 3, 4, 5 and 6 letters. Each word is written along a winding path that steps
up, down, left or right (never diagonally), and every open tile belongs to exactly
one word, so the four paths tile the whole board.

One deterministic puzzle a day, the same for everyone, seeded from the date in
Yerevan time.
